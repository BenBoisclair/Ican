# What we need for three solution pages

Pages: **IT Security Assessment**, **Enterprise Wi-Fi**, **IT Consulting & Assessment**.
The old website had nothing on these, so every fact has to come from the team. Short answers and bullet points are fine: we will write the page copy and send it back for approval.

Until these arrive, the three cards on the homepage open the contact form with that solution already picked.

## For all three pages

1. **The promise.** In one sentence, what does the client walk away with? (Example: "A ranked list of your security gaps and what each one costs to fix.")
2. **Who it's for.** The typical client (industry, size, number of sites) and the moment they call us (audit coming, new office, Wi-Fi complaints, before a big purchase).
3. **What's included.** A checklist of what we look at or do. One line per item.
4. **The steps.** From the first call to the final handover: each step, roughly how long it takes, on site or remote, and what the client has to provide (access, a contact person, downtime).
5. **What the client receives.** The report, risk ratings, heat maps, roadmap, presentation. **Please send one sample page with client names blacked out** — it is the most convincing thing we can show.
6. **Tools and standards.** The tools we use and the standards we follow (e.g. ISO 27001, CIS, NIST, vendor best practice).
7. **Team.** Certifications of the people who do this work, and their years of experience.
8. **Proof.** 1–3 real projects. For each: the client or industry, what we found or built, and a number (sites, devices, users, issues found, money saved, downtime avoided). Tell us whether we may name the client.
9. **Photos.** 3–6 real photos of this work: on site, equipment, the team, a report on screen. Landscape, full resolution, nothing confidential visible.
10. **Brands.** Vendors we partner with or are certified on, for this solution.
11. **Price.** How it's priced: fixed fee, per site, per device, "from X baht", or quote after a call. Is the first consultation free?
12. **Questions clients ask.** The 3–5 questions clients ask most, with our answers.
13. **What comes next.** What we offer after it's done (fixes, installation, maintenance, support contract).

## IT Security Assessment, also tell us

- Which assessment types we offer: vulnerability scan, penetration test, firewall and configuration review, Wi-Fi security, server hardening review, policy or compliance gap review, phishing test.
- What we test: firewalls, servers, Wi-Fi, PCs, cloud, websites.
- How findings are rated (e.g. critical, high, medium, low) and whether a retest after fixes is included.
- How client data is protected: NDA, who sees the results, what we keep afterwards.
- Which regulations clients ask about: PDPA, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, or head office audits.

## Enterprise Wi-Fi, also tell us

- Services: site survey (on plan or on site), design, installation, tuning, fixing an existing network, ongoing management.
- Access point and controller brands we install.
- Our largest Wi-Fi job: number of sites, access points, users, floor area.
- Places we have done it: offices, warehouses, factories, hotels, condos, schools.
- What we measure and hand over: coverage, speed, roaming, heat maps (a sample heat map would be ideal).
- Guest Wi-Fi, login pages and security options.
- Support after installation: response time, maintenance contract.

## IT Consulting & Assessment, also tell us

- Areas we assess: network, server room, security, software, cloud, IT team and processes.
- What the roadmap contains: priorities, budget estimates, timeline.
- How long a typical engagement takes, and who does it (seniority, years of experience).
- Whether we are brand-neutral, or recommend brands we sell.
- One example where an assessment changed a client's decision: money saved, outage avoided, or a better plan.
